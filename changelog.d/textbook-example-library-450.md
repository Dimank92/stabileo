### Added
#### Textbook example library: 300+ solved problems across structural analysis & design (#450)

Expanded the automated textbook example library to over 300 automated validation tests, covering classical problems from standard textbooks:
- `mccormac_steel`: AISC 360-16 / McCormac & Csernak structural steel design (tension members, block shear, column buckling, compact/LTB beam flexure, shear yielding, deflection serviceability, combined axial-flexure interaction, bolted and welded connections, base plates).
- `mccormac_rc`: ACI 318 / McCormac & Brown reinforced concrete design (cracked/uncracked section properties, Whitney stress block flexure, minimum/balanced steel, T-beams, doubly reinforced beams, Branson effective inertia, development length, stirrups, tied/spiral columns, slender column moment magnification, punching shear, retaining walls).
- `hibbeler_influence_lines`: Hibbeler structural analysis influence lines for determinate beams, floor girders, Pratt/Warren/Howe trusses, Müller-Breslau principle, two-span continuous beams, and propped cantilevers.
- `gere_goodno_mechanics`: Gere & Goodno mechanics of materials (thermal stress, stepped bars, shaft torsion, beam flexure and shear flow, Mohr's circle plane stress, pressure vessels, elastic deflections, indeterminate beams, Euler buckling for 4 boundary conditions, Castigliano energy).
- `kassimali_matrix_3d`: Kassimali 3D space trusses, regular tetrahedron, transmission tower bays, space frames, and grids.

Every problem cites textbook editions, chapter sections, and analytical solutions with strict tolerances.
