### Added
#### Certificates for nonlinear analyses (co-rotational, material, fiber) (#502)

While linear static, modal, buckling, P-Delta, harmonic, and plastic collapse results have rigorous certificates, nonlinear solvers were previously benchmarked without machine-checked certificates. Added rigorous a-posteriori certificates for nonlinear analyses (co-rotational large displacements, material-nonlinear elastoplastic, and fiber beam-column analyses) using Newton-Kantorovich residual inclusion with interval tangent coercivity evaluation.
