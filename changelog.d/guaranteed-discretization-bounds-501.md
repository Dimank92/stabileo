### Added
#### Guaranteed a-posteriori upper bounds on discretization error (2026-10-28)

Discretization error estimation now includes Ainsworth–Oden equilibrated residual estimators
providing guaranteed mathematical upper bounds on the energy-norm error for linear elasticity
on supported elements (plates, quads, quad9s, curved shells, and solid-shells), bounding the true
continuum error rather than only providing asymptotic recovery estimates.
